Prototype · not a live service

Making the job market in Milan more accessible for English-speaking international students.

Gigomy is being designed by a team of students. It explores how a small, trusted platform could connect international students with Milan businesses looking for flexible, English-speaking help.

Why we're building this

Our motives

01

The language barrier

Many international students arrive in Milan without fluent Italian. Most part-time listings assume it, so capable students are filtered out before they can apply.

02

Scattered opportunities

English-friendly roles do exist, but they're spread across group chats, forums and word of mouth. There's no single trusted place to find them.

03

Small businesses can't reach students

Cafés, startups and studios want short-term help but have no easy way to reach motivated students. Large career portals are built for corporate recruiting.

04

Learning by building

As a student project, this is also how we practise strategy: validating a real problem, designing a solution and testing it with users.

What guides us

Built on four principles

  • Student-firstFree for students and built around lecture timetables and exam seasons.
  • Trust-basedVerified students and employers reviewed by the team.
  • Discovery onlyWe connect people. Applications, contracts and pay stay between student and employer.
  • Non-profitUsefulness comes first. No fees and no paid placements.

This website is a prototype

It's a design blueprint to show how the product could look and work. Every company, job, applicant number, profile and map location is simulated. Sign-ups, applications and verification aren't connected to anything yet, and no personal data is collected.

Gigomy is an independent student initiative.
